Final year project

A full system I built with my team: AI, backend, web and mobile in one product.

Trash Tracker

A smart, AI-powered waste detection and dispatching system. It scans satellite imagery with a custom-trained YOLOv8 model, finds waste accumulation, converts detections into GPS coordinates and sends field workers to clean them up, with proof photos for accountability. First rollout area: Muzaffarabad. Group project, supervised at UAJK.
